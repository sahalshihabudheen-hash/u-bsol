📖 Our Story

How U-BSOL Came to Be

U-BSOL was founded by Shihabudheen Uruniyan in 2020, born out of a decade-long journey of building international IT projects and seeing a clear gap in the market.

We are a group of technical professionals who started working together from 2011 to the present. We have successfully completed numerous domestic and international IT projects across system analysis, design and development, deployment and server management.

From 2015, we witnessed the e-commerce sector boom dramatically. We focused on e-commerce projects, developed platforms using various technologies including open-source. Our customers always needed more — advanced features within their budget.

That challenge inspired us to build our own platform — U-BSOL — a complete, affordable, white-label business solution that we wish existed when we started.
