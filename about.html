Who Are We?

U-BSOL - Your Business Solution Partner

U-BSOL is an Indian information technology company that delivers complete solution for business automation and digitalization for any level of business. U-BSOL was founded by Shihabudheen Uruniyan in 2020 for implementing the innovative ideas for the business automation by the help of modern information technology. U-BSOL is a collection of applications for various needs which contain Billing software, Mobile applications, Web applications, Tracking applications, Market place, etc.

We provide a comprehensive platform that provides widespread digital solutions to all business ventures seeking for digital transformation into the modern World. We never compromise on the technology, performance and quality of our application. We will frequently add many services and features in upcoming releases. Our primary aim is to provide a complete features rich solution into public and help them to grow their business with every affordable price.

Our Story

We are a group of technical professionals who started work together from 2011 till present. We have successfully completed lot of domestic and international IT projects in various aspects.
