Our Story

Built by Developers,
For Business Owners

U-BSOL is an Indian information technology company that delivers complete solutions for business automation and digitalization at any level. Founded in 2020 by Shihabudheen Uruniyan, the company was born from a simple but powerful idea — every small business deserves enterprise-grade digital tools.

We are a group of technical professionals who have worked together since 2011, successfully completing numerous domestic and international IT projects across system analysis, design and development, deployment and server management.

When we saw our customers always seeking advanced features beyond their budget, we took action. Instead of compromising, we built our own platform — one that delivers every feature at a price any business can afford.

Our Journey

Milestones That
Define Us

2011 — The Beginning
Team Formation

A dedicated group of IT professionals began working together, building expertise across system analysis, design, development, and deployment through dozens of challenging international projects.

2015 — Pivoting to E-Commerce
Witnessing the E-Commerce Boom

The team shifted focus to the rapidly growing e-commerce sector, developing multiple projects using various technologies including open-source platforms, building deep domain expertise.

2020 — Foundation
U-BSOL Is Born

Frustrated by the gap between what customers needed and what they could afford, Shihabudheen Uruniyan officially founded U-BSOL — a one-stop SaaS platform for business automation.

2021–2023 — Growth
Platform Expansion

Rapid product development saw the addition of mobile apps, billing POS systems, booking platforms, tracking apps, and multiple payment gateway integrations serving hundreds of businesses.

2024–2026 — Today
700+ Customers & Growing

U-BSOL now serves 700+ satisfied customers across India with an ever-growing suite of features, dedicated mobile apps, and a commitment to continuous innovation and support.
