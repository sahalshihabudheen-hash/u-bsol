Enquiry Module (RFQ)

An enquiry form associated directly with your product and service detail pages. This enables customers to submit enquiries for single or multiple items, facilitating seamless communication between customer and merchant. Merchants can then adjust pricing dynamically as per client specifications and convert enquiries directly into processed orders. This is ideal for special customers and B2B-related sales workflows.

User Group & Access Roles

Manage your business operations in a more structured and secure manner. This module allows you to set up multiple user groups for your internal team (Admin users) with customized, granular access levels and permissions, ensuring every manager or operator sees exactly what they need.

Customer Groups

Organize and segment your customer base into custom categories (e.g., family groups, coworkers, wholesale clients, or VIP friends). This module empowers you to assign different pricing rules and discounts for products or services based on the specific group the logged-in customer belongs to.
