Got Questions?

Affiliate Program FAQ

If you don't see an answer to your question, you can send us an email from our contact form.

How can I become an Affiliate partner? +
It's easy! Fill the application form on this page. Once you submit the form, you will be redirected to your dashboard where you need to complete your profile and update your KYC details. After that our team will process your application and notify you once it is approved.
Who can become an Affiliate partner? +
Anyone! We welcome developers, designers, freelancers, bloggers, influencers, marketing agencies, businessmen, professionals, consultants, and more.
How does the affiliate program work? +
After becoming our affiliate partner we will provide you an affiliate ID associated with U-BSOL different package links. You can share those links within your friend circle, community, social media, etc. You will be rewarded an affiliate commission if anyone purchases our package through your shared links.
How much can I earn as a U-BSOL affiliate partner? +
You can easily earn from 15% up to 25% commissions from every paid customer that you referred. There is no cap on the number of referrals you can make.
How do I know about my referred customers? +
All your referred customers' details will be listed in your dashboard. You can see this under the buddies list in your dashboard.
How do affiliate payments work? +
Payment information will be listed in your dashboard immediately after the referred customer completes their invoice payment. We will also send you a notification. The affiliate commission amount will be transferred to your bank account within 48 hours after closing the invoice.
Who is there to support me? +
Our supporting team and technical team will work for you as your team. We will help you and guide you to understand our product and services in more detail. If you have any question regarding our application, we are here for you.
Can I refer myself? +
No. You cannot refer yourself and you will not receive a commission on your own accounts. Self-referrals, fraudulent transactions, and suspected affiliate fraud will result in account termination.
