Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need technical knowledge to use U-BSOL? →
No! U-BSOL is designed for non-technical users. Everything is manageable through an intuitive dashboard with clicks.
Will the app be under my brand name? →
Yes, absolutely. Your website, mobile app and all customer-facing elements will carry your brand name and logo.
Which payment gateways are supported? →
PayTM, PayUMoney, PayPal, RazorPay and Stripe are all pre-integrated. Just add your credentials and start collecting payments.
Can I upgrade my plan later? →
Yes, you can upgrade your plan at any time as your business grows. Contact our support team to assist you.
Is there a free trial available? →
Please contact us to discuss a demo or trial arrangement tailored to your specific business needs.
What kind of support do you offer? →
24/7 online ticket support, training videos on your dashboard, help documents and regular webinars for new features.
