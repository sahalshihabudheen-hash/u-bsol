What is U-BSOL ? +
U-BSOL is an Indian information technology company specializing in business automation and digitalization. We offer Software as a Service (SaaS) business applications, including ERP, CRM, inventory, and online marketplace solutions designed to help merchants digitize their operations.
What is U-BSOL market place? +
The U-BSOL Marketplace is a centralized digital commerce platform where multiple merchants can list their products and services. It connects buyers with various vendors under a unified marketplace environment, enabling multi-vendor transactions, logistics, and payments.
What makes you different from other companies? +
We focus on complete brand ownership and cost-effective automation. With U-BSOL, merchants get their own custom domain, unique branding, and a secure cloud interface with zero hosting complications. Our platform is continuously upgraded by our dedicated R&D team to deliver premium features without expensive custom development costs.
What is SAAS modal business in simple terms? +
SaaS stands for Software as a Service. In simple terms, it is a licensing model where software is hosted on cloud servers and accessed over the internet. Instead of buying expensive hardware or software licenses upfront, you pay a flexible subscription fee (monthly/annually) to use the latest version of our platform.
What is Cloud computing ? And what are the benefits ? +
Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services (servers, storage, databases, networking, software) over the internet. Key benefits include zero server maintenance or upgrade costs, accessibility from any device globally, high-level data security, automated backups, and the ability to scale up business resources instantly.
How do I register as U-BSOL merchant? +
You can register by choosing your desired plan on our Packages page and filling out the onboarding form. Our team will review your business information, verify your requirements, and help set up your custom store.
How do I register as merchant in U-BSOL market place? +
After registering as a standard U-BSOL merchant, you can opt-in to list your store and inventory on the shared U-BSOL marketplace. This enables you to receive organic traffic and orders directly from the marketplace portal in addition to your standalone storefront.
Does U-BSOL market place is free ? +
Accessing and setting up your basic marketplace presence is included in standard U-BSOL packages. However, additional payment gateway transaction charges, commission structures, or advanced catalog tools may vary depending on your chosen subscription tier.
Can I use my own custom domain? +
Yes, absolutely! With U-BSOL, you get complete brand ownership. You can easily point your own custom domain (e.g., yourstore.com) to our secure cloud servers, and your clients will never see any U-BSOL branding.
How secure is U-BSOL? +
We implement bank-grade security protocols. All connections are encrypted via SSL, customer transaction details are tokenized, and database backups are performed automatically daily. We protect your client data with advanced firewall protections on our secure cloud architecture.
What is the refund policy for packages? +
We offer a transparent return and refund policy. If you cancel your subscription, we calculate the usage fees up to the end of the billing cycle, and refund any excess advance payments according to our terms and conditions.
How does payment gateway integration work? +
We support major local and international payment gateways, including UPI, Razorpay, Stripe, and debit/credit cards. You can connect your own merchant account keys to receive customer payments directly into your bank account.
Is there a commission on trading transactions? +
For retail and wholesale trading setups, transaction commission structures depend on your package. Most premium packages offer 0% platform transaction commission, meaning you keep 100% of your sales profits.
How do I manage daily store operations? +
U-BSOL provides an all-in-one administrative dashboard. You can manage orders, view analytics reports, update product catalogs, track delivery boys, and handle customer queries from any desktop or mobile device.
How does the U-BSOL Affiliate Program pay out? +
Our affiliate partners earn 15%–25% commissions on referrals. Commissions are tracked on your affiliate dashboard and payouts are processed directly to your bank account within 48 hours of your buddy completing their subscription invoice.
