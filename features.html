Checkout & Delivery Methods

U-BSOL provides full control on checkout and delivery operations. You can enable or disable these operations any time as per your needs. We enable multiple variations of checkouts to minimize friction.

We provide 3 types of delivery methods for different usages: Pickup from store, Delivery to address for online customers, and Store delivery for your walk-in customers.

U-BSOL sends various automated notifications based on delivery status, such as "Product ready for delivery", "Under transit", and "Delivered".
