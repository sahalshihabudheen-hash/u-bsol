FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Can I upgrade my plan later? +
Yes! You can upgrade to any higher plan at any time. Contact our support team and we'll handle the migration seamlessly without losing any of your existing data or settings.
Is my brand name used on the platform? +
Absolutely. Your website, mobile app, and all customer touchpoints use your brand name, logo, and colors. U-BSOL operates invisibly in the background — we power your brand, not replace it.
What payment methods do you support? +
We support PayTM, PayUmoney, PayPal, RazorPay & Stripe — all pre-integrated. You just connect your billing account credentials. Cash on Delivery is also supported and can be enabled per product.
How is the app published in the Play Store? +
For Starter and Premier plans, your branded Android app is submitted to the Play Store under your business account. We handle the entire technical process — you just provide your branding assets.
Do I need technical knowledge to manage my store? +
Not at all. U-BSOL is designed for business owners, not developers. Everything is managed through an intuitive admin dashboard with video tutorials included in every plan. No coding required.
What happens to my data if I cancel? +
Your data belongs to you. If you cancel, we provide a full export of your products, customer data, and orders. You have 30 days after cancellation to download your data before it is removed from our servers.
